# Is It Wrong to Try to Pick Up Girls in a Dungeon? (DanMachi)

## About the Series

**DanMachi** is a fantasy action anime based on the light novel series by *Fujino Omori*. The anime is produced by **J.C.Staff**, and the first season aired in 2015.

> "A hero is someone who never gives up, no matter how hard things get."

(This is a paraphrase of the show's spirit, not an official quote.)

---

## Story Summary

The story takes place in the city of **Orario**, which sits on top of a huge Dungeon. Adventurers go into the Dungeon to fight monsters, earn money, and grow stronger. **Bell Cranel**, a young and inexperienced adventurer, joins the Hestia Familia and trains to become a strong hero.

### Main Characters

1. Bell Cranel
2. Hestia
3. Aiz Wallenstein
4. Lili
5. Welf

### Why I Like It

- Great action and fight scenes
- Bell's growth feels satisfying
- Lovable characters and a fun fantasy world
- Nice mix of comedy, adventure, and emotion
